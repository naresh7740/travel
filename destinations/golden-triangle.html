A closer look

A classic North India circuit

The Golden Triangle links Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, bringing together historic monuments, lively markets and distinctive city experiences.

Choose the order and length of each stop around your travel dates, interests and preferred pace.

Three memorable stops

Along the route

Delhi

Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate and Lodhi Garden.

Agra

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Itmad-ud-Daulah and the nearby heritage site of Fatehpur Sikri.

Jaipur

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Jaigarh Fort and Nahargarh Fort.

When to go

October to March is generally suggested for many Golden Triangle visits.

Good to know

Allow time for intercity transfers and check monument hours and visitor rules for your dates.
