A closer look

Heritage, hills and fresh air

Shimla is Himachal Pradesh's capital and a popular base for exploring the surrounding hills. Walk the historic centre, visit viewpoints and add nearby places such as Kufri, Mashobra or Chail to your plans.

Some attractions are outside the town centre, so allow time for local transport and check seasonal road and weather conditions.

Town, viewpoints & day trips

Places to explore in Shimla

The Ridge & Mall Road

Walk the Ridge, Scandal Point, Mall Road, Lakkar Bazaar and the Gaiety Theatre area.

Jakhoo Temple

Visit the hilltop Hanuman temple and its surrounding forest, reached by foot, taxi or ropeway.

Viceregal Lodge

See the historic building now known as Rashtrapati Niwas, its gardens and the Indian Institute of Advanced Study.

Christ Church & State Museum

Explore Christ Church on the Ridge and the Himachal State Museum's regional art and heritage collections.

Annandale

A forest-ringed open ground with the Army Heritage Museum and golf course area.

Kufri

Enjoy mountain viewpoints, seasonal snow activities and the Himalayan Nature Park.

Fagu

A peaceful highland stop known for orchard landscapes, mountain views and winter scenery.

Green Valley

A popular roadside viewpoint for forested slopes and mountain scenery on the Kufri route.

Mashobra

Explore forest trails, the Reserve Forest Sanctuary area and Craignano Nature Park.

Naldehra

Visit the Naldehra Golf Course, cedar forests and nearby Shaily Peak viewpoints.

Chadwick Falls

A seasonal waterfall and forest walk near Summer Hill; check local trail and weather conditions.

Tara Devi Temple

A hilltop temple with views across the surrounding hills, accessible by road and a short walk.

Kalka–Shimla Toy Train

Ride the heritage mountain railway through tunnels, bridges and hill scenery. Check current timetable and availability.

Chail

A nearby day trip for Chail Palace, the cricket ground and forest walks around Chail Wildlife Sanctuary.

When to go

March to June is popular for pleasant hill weather; December to February can bring cold and occasional snow. Conditions vary each year.

Good to know

Check attraction opening days, toy train schedules, road conditions and weather before travel. Nearby day trips may need a full day.
