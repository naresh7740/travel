A closer look

Big landscapes, thoughtful travel

Leh is a gateway to dramatic Himalayan terrain, historic monasteries and remote valleys. A well-paced plan matters: altitude, road conditions and seasonal access all shape the journey.

Build in time to adjust to the altitude and confirm the status of each route before setting out.

Places to explore

Leh highlights

Leh town

Leh Palace, Shanti Stupa, Main Bazaar and the nearby monasteries of Thiksey and Hemis.

Nubra Valley

Diskit Monastery, Hunder sand dunes, Turtuk and the route over Khardung La.

Pangong Lake

Explore lakeside viewpoints and nearby villages; access depends on permits and conditions.

Tso Moriri

A remote high-altitude lake in the Changthang region. Check road access and permits before travel.

Sham Valley

Visit Alchi Monastery, Likir Monastery, the Magnetic Hill area and Indus Valley viewpoints.

Mountain passes

Chang La and other high passes are seasonal routes. Confirm current road and weather conditions.

When to go

June to September is the main road-trip season for many routes. Exact access depends on current conditions.

Good to know

Altitude acclimatisation, permits and road access need careful planning. Confirm current guidance before booking.
