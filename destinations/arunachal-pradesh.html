Eastern Himalayas · Arunachal Pradesh

A quieter side of the Himalayas.

Travel through mountain valleys, high passes and remote cultural landscapes in India's northeast.

Plan an Arunachal trip

A closer look

Take the scenic route

Arunachal Pradesh is known for dramatic scenery and long, winding routes through the Eastern Himalayas. Towns and valleys along the way offer a memorable view of the region's landscapes and culture.

Travel plans should allow extra time for road conditions and required permits.

Places to explore

Arunachal highlights

Tawang

Tawang Monastery, Tawang War Memorial, Urgelling Monastery and the surrounding mountain viewpoints.

Dirang

Dirang Dzong, Sangti Valley, hot springs and the scenic route toward Tawang.

Sela Pass

Sela Lake, high mountain scenery and the pass road between Dirang and Tawang.

Bomdila

Bomdila Monastery, local craft centre and viewpoints across the Himalayan ranges.

Ziro Valley

Talley Valley, pine-covered landscapes, local villages and the Ziro plateau.

Mechuka

A remote valley known for mountain scenery, the Samten Yongcha Monastery and riverside views.

When to go

October to April is suggested for many circuits, but weather varies by area and elevation.

Good to know

Permit requirements apply. Confirm current rules, route conditions and local guidance before departure.
