A closer look

A state of many contrasts

From historic cities and temples to wildlife and the wide-open Rann, Gujarat rewards travellers who enjoy variety. Pick a regional focus or connect destinations over a longer trip.

Seasonal events and access differ by location, so plan around confirmed dates and current local conditions.

Places to explore

Gujarat highlights

Rann of Kutch

White Rann, Kala Dungar, Dholavira and the seasonal Rann Utsav area.

Dwarka & Somnath

Dwarkadhish Temple, Bet Dwarka, Nageshwar Jyotirlinga and Somnath Temple.

Ahmedabad

Sabarmati Ashram, Adalaj Stepwell, Sidi Saiyyed Mosque and the old city heritage area.

Statue of Unity

Statue of Unity, Valley of Flowers, Sardar Sarovar Dam viewpoints and nearby attractions.

Gir National Park

Asiatic lion habitat and forest safaris. Safari permits and seasonal opening dates apply.

Modhera & Patan

Sun Temple at Modhera and Rani Ki Vav, the historic stepwell at Patan.

Coastal Gujarat

Explore Mandvi Beach, Vijay Vilas Palace and coastal places around the Kutch region.

When to go

October to March is generally suitable for many circuits. Rann event dates and access vary each year.

Good to know

Check current permits, event schedules and travel requirements for the areas on your route.
