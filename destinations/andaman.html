A closer look

Sea, sand and slow days

Andaman is an island escape for travellers drawn to the sea. Port Blair is a common starting point, with island visits and beach time shaped around your dates and available connections.

Make room for unhurried coastlines, time on the water and a gentler pace between places.

Places to explore

Island highlights

Port Blair

Cellular Jail, Corbyn's Cove Beach, Marina Park and Chatham Saw Mill.

Swaraj Dweep (Havelock)

Radhanagar Beach, Elephant Beach and Kala Pathar Beach.

Shaheed Dweep (Neil)

Bharatpur Beach, Laxmanpur Beach and the Natural Bridge shoreline.

Baratang Island

Limestone Caves, mangrove creeks and the mud volcano area. Check access arrangements before travel.

Ross & North Bay

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Island and North Bay are popular short excursions from Port Blair.

When to go

October to May is generally suggested for many island trips. Confirm current weather, ferry schedules and local conditions before booking.

Good to know

Island transfers and permits may affect the plan. Check current access and transport availability for your travel dates.
