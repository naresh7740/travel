A closer look

Between lakes and mountains

Kashmir brings together lakeside stays, gardens, mountain towns and scenic drives. Srinagar can anchor a trip, with the wider route adapted to your pace and season.

Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg are possible additions, depending on weather, access and the time available.

Places to explore

Kashmir highlights

Srinagar

Dal Lake, Mughal Gardens, Shankaracharya Temple, Pari Mahal and houseboat stays.

Gulmarg

Gulmarg Gondola, meadows, ski slopes and mountain viewpoints.

Pahalgam

Lidder River, Betaab Valley, Aru Valley and nearby pine-covered trails.

Sonamarg

Thajiwas Glacier viewpoints and Sind River scenery; access can be seasonal.

Doodhpathri & Yusmarg

Quiet meadows, streams and mountain scenery on day trips from Srinagar.

Katra (route extension)

A common pilgrimage stop for Vaishno Devi, planned as a separate route from the Kashmir Valley.

When to go

March to October suits many valley visits; winter is popular for snow experiences. Conditions change by area and month.

Good to know

Road conditions and local access can change. Confirm the latest route, weather and travel guidance before departure.
