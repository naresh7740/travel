Lake walks and wooded hills

Kodaikanal's compact centre is surrounded by forested slopes, viewpoints and walking routes. Keep time for relaxed exploration rather than trying to cover every stop in one day.

Rain and fog can affect visibility and trail conditions. Check local notices and choose safe routes for your group.

Places to explore in Kodaikanal

Kodai Lake

Walk or cycle around the star-shaped lake and check which boating services are operating.

Coaker's Walk

Enjoy a short ridge walk when open; views depend on cloud cover and weather.

Bryant Park

Visit the botanical garden and seasonal flower displays; confirm current timings.

Pillar Rocks & Guna Caves area

Check access rules and weather before visiting the viewpoints and forest-side attractions.

Walks & viewpoints

Wear comfortable shoes, stay on marked paths and avoid exposed viewpoints during poor weather.

Local transport
