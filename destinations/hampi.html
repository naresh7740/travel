Monuments in a dramatic landscape

Hampi's monuments are spread across a broad heritage landscape. Plan visits by area, carry water and allow time to move between sites.

Respect monument rules and protected areas. Check current opening hours, access and local transport before your visit.

Virupaksha Temple

Visit the active temple and surrounding bazaar area while following local visitor etiquette.

Vittala Temple Complex

Explore the celebrated monument complex and stone chariot; check current entry arrangements.

Hampi Bazaar & Hemakuta Hill

Tungabhadra River & Matanga Hill

Enjoy river scenery and hill views; check safe access and avoid exposed trails in extreme heat.

Walk prepared

Wear sturdy footwear, carry drinking water and plan outdoor visits for cooler parts of the day.

Heritage care

Do not climb or mark protected structures. Follow signage and any site-specific visitor restrictions.
