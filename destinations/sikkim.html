Mountain journeys at your own pace

Sikkim offers very different landscapes between its towns, valleys and high mountain routes. Gangtok makes a convenient starting point for many itineraries.

Some border and protected-area destinations require permits, and access can change with weather or administration directions. Confirm requirements before departure.
