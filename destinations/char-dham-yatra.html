The four shrines

A sacred journey through Garhwal

The Uttarakhand Char Dham circuit connects four high-altitude Hindu pilgrimage sites: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. The commonly followed sequence starts at Yamunotri and continues to Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.

Road travel, steep walking routes and changing mountain weather make this a demanding journey. The details below are a planning guide, not a live road or opening-status update.

Photos, altitude & access

Explore the four Dhams

Yamunotri

Dedicated to Goddess Yamuna; Surya Kund and Divya Shila are important nearby sites. The temple is about 3,293 m above sea level. The usual road journey ends at Janki Chatti, followed by an approximately 6 km uphill trek one way.

Photo: Tanusree Das · CC BY-SA 4.0

Gangotri

Dedicated to Goddess Ganga, this road-accessible shrine stands near the Bhagirathi River at about 3,100 m. Nearby places include Harsil, Bhairon Ghati and Gaumukh; Gaumukh requires a separate permitted trek.

Photo: Arupamdas · CC BY-SA 4.0

Kedarnath

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, at about 3,580 m. From Gaurikund, the traditional route involves an approximately 16 km trek one way. Helicopter services and trek arrangements depend on current rules and weather.

Photo: Shaq774 · Public domain

Badrinath

Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, at about 3,133 m and reached by road via Joshimath. Visit Tapt Kund, Brahma Kapal and, when access permits, Mana village and nearby viewpoints.

Photo: Vishwanath Negi · CC BY 4.0

Common route from Haridwar / Rishikesh

Yatra sequence & connections

  1. 1 · YamunotriHaridwar/Rishikesh → Barkot → Janki Chatti → about 6 km trek to the temple.
  2. 2 · GangotriContinue via Uttarkashi and Harsil; Gangotri is generally reached by road.
  3. 3 · KedarnathVia Uttarkashi, Rudraprayag and Guptkashi to Sonprayag/Gaurikund, then about 16 km trek.
  4. 4 · BadrinathTravel via Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Joshimath to Badrinath.

Road distances and travel times vary by starting point, route restrictions, traffic and weather. Confirm the current route and road status locally before departure.

Example only · adjust to current conditions

Sample 12-day plan

  1. Day 1Arrive in Haridwar or Rishikesh; rest and complete registration checks.
  2. Day 2Drive to Barkot and stay overnight.
  3. Day 3Travel to Janki Chatti, trek to Yamunotri and return to the planned stay.
  4. Day 4Transfer toward Uttarkashi; allow time for mountain roads.
  5. Day 5Visit Gangotri and return or stay as arranged.
  6. Day 6Travel toward Guptkashi/Sonprayag; keep a flexible buffer.
  7. Day 7Reach Gaurikund and begin the Kedarnath trek; overnight arrangements depend on availability.
  8. Day 8Darshan and return trek as conditions, health and local instructions allow.
  9. Day 9Rest and recover near Guptkashi or another confirmed stop.
  10. Day 10Drive toward Joshimath and confirm the latest onward road status.
  11. Day 11Visit Badrinath and nearby places that are open and accessible.
  12. Day 12Return toward Rishikesh/Haridwar; add extra days if needed.

This is not a guaranteed package itinerary. Do not compress rest or travel time; change the plan for weather, altitude response, road closures and official directions.

Before you travel

Registration & safety checklist

Official registration

The Uttarakhand government portal says pilgrims and vehicles must be registered and pilgrims verified at the shrines. Register through the official portal, carry the Yatra Registration Letter/QR and check that year's requirements.

Official Char Dham registration portal ↗

Health & altitude

These are high-altitude routes with long drives and strenuous walks. Speak with a doctor before travel, especially for heart, lung or mobility concerns. Carry prescribed medicines and do not ignore altitude symptoms.

Pack for changing weather

Carry warm layers, rain protection, sturdy walking shoes, water, valid photo ID and essential medicines. Keep buffer days for delays and follow local administration instructions.

Helicopter services

Services are weather- and permit-dependent. The official registration portal directs pilgrims to book Kedarnath heli services through IRCTC only.

Official IRCTC HeliYatra ↗

Season & opening dates

The shrines operate seasonally and may close during winter. Opening/closing dates and access are announced or updated by authorities; verify the current year's dates and advisories before booking.
