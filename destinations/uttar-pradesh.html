A closer look

Stories around every turn

Uttar Pradesh brings together Mughal-era landmarks, historic cities and important pilgrimage destinations. Build your trip around Agra, Varanasi or a wider circuit.

Give each place enough time: the region's heritage, riverfronts and local traditions reward a thoughtful pace.

Places to explore

Uttar Pradesh highlights

Agra

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Itmad-ud-Daulah and the nearby heritage city of Fatehpur Sikri.

Varanasi

Dashashwamedh Ghat, Assi Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath Temple and the old city's lanes.

Mathura & Vrindavan

Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, Banke Bihari Temple, Prem Mandir and the ghats of the Braj region.

Ayodhya

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan and Ram Ki Paidi.

Prayagraj

Triveni Sangam, Anand Bhavan, Allahabad Fort surroundings and the city's historic ghats.

Lucknow

Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara, Rumi Darwaza and the historic market areas.

When to go

October to March is generally suggested for many visits, with milder conditions for sightseeing.

Good to know

Opening hours, visitor rules and local arrangements can vary. Check current details for each landmark before travel.
