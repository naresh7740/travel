A closer look

Waterfalls and green valleys

Cherrapunji, also known as Sohra, is a highland base for waterfalls, caves and village walks. Conditions vary considerably by season and rainfall.

Check local weather, trail access and road conditions before visiting viewpoints or taking part in outdoor activities.

Waterfalls, caves & trails

Places to explore in Cherrapunji

Nohkalikai Falls

Visit the viewpoint when open and keep a safe distance from cliff edges.

Seven Sisters Falls

Visibility is strongly affected by weather; check conditions before making the drive.

Mawsmai Cave

Follow entry guidance and wear suitable footwear on damp or uneven cave paths.

Nongriat Living-Root Bridge

The steep stair route takes time and fitness; start early and arrange a safe return before dark.

Rainy conditions

Carry rain protection, expect slippery paths and follow local advice about waterfalls and river levels.

Trail planning

Assess the distance and stairs before starting a walk, carry water and use local guides where appropriate.
