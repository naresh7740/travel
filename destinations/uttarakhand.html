A closer look

Make room for the outdoors

Uttarakhand offers a wide choice of hill towns, river valleys and pilgrimage routes. A trip can be restful, adventurous or a combination of places that suit your interests.

Seasonal access differs across the state, especially for higher routes and pilgrimage destinations.

Places to explore

Uttarakhand highlights

Mussoorie

Gun Hill, Kempty Falls, Lal Tibba, Company Garden and Camel's Back Road.

Rishikesh

Lakshman Jhula area, Ram Jhula, Triveni Ghat, Beatles Ashram and riverfront experiences.

Nainital

Naini Lake, Naina Devi Temple, Snow View Point, Tiffin Top and nearby Bhimtal.

Haridwar

Har Ki Pauri, Mansa Devi Temple, Chandi Devi Temple and the evening Ganga Aarti.

Jim Corbett

Corbett Tiger Reserve zones, Ramganga reservoir and nearby nature stays. Safari permits are required.

Auli

Ski slopes, Auli Artificial Lake, Gorson Bugyal and views toward Nanda Devi.

Dehradun & Mussoorie foothills

Robber's Cave, Sahastradhara, Forest Research Institute and nearby hill viewpoints.

Char Dham Yatra

Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath. View the route, shrine details and registration guidance.

When to go

March to June and September to November suit many trips. Pilgrimage seasons and high-altitude access vary.

Good to know

Check current road, weather and pilgrimage information, especially when travelling to higher elevations.
