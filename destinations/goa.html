Coast, culture and local flavour

Goa's beach towns and historic neighbourhoods each have a different pace. Choose a base around the experiences you want and allow time for local transport.

Beach conditions and water activities are seasonal. Follow lifeguard flags and local safety guidance, and check opening hours for heritage sites.

Beaches, towns & heritage

Places to explore in Goa

North Goa beaches

Explore the Calangute, Baga, Anjuna and Vagator areas, each with its own atmosphere.

South Goa

Consider Palolem, Agonda and nearby coastal villages for a slower beach stay.

Panaji & Fontainhas

Walk the capital's riverside areas and colourful heritage lanes.

Old Goa

Visit historic churches and basilicas; check visitor rules and service schedules before arrival.

Getting around

Distances can take longer than expected during busy periods. Plan transport in advance, especially for evening journeys.

Coastal safety

Swim only where permitted, follow lifeguard advice and avoid entering the water during rough conditions.
