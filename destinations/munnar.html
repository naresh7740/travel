Tea gardens and highland scenery

Munnar is a hill-town base for tea-estate scenery, nature areas and winding mountain roads. Choose a few highlights each day and allow time for travel between them.

Park entry, road access and weather can change. Check current notices and operating hours before making day-trip plans.
