Waterfalls, forests and living roots

Meghalaya's journeys connect Shillong, Sohra (Cherrapunji), river valleys and rural communities. Travel times can be long on hill roads, so leave space between stops.

Rainfall, river conditions and trail access vary by season. Check local guidance before walks, cave visits or river activities.

Places to explore in Meghalaya

Shillong

Explore the hill capital, local markets, viewpoints and nearby waterfalls.

Sohra (Cherrapunji)

Visit waterfalls, caves and viewpoints; see the separate Cherrapunji guide for more.

Dawki & the Umngot River

River activities depend on water and weather conditions; use local authorized operators.

Nongriat Living-Root Bridge

Reach the bridge by a steep stair trail; assess fitness and allow enough daylight for the return.

Rain & trails

Paths can be slippery in wet weather. Check local conditions and choose walks that match your group's ability.

Respect local communities

Follow village guidance, keep natural areas clean and arrange local transport and guides responsibly.
