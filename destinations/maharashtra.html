A closer look

Choose your own Maharashtra

Maharashtra offers very different experiences across its cities, hill stations, heritage sites and pilgrimage destinations. A trip can focus on one region or connect a few stops.

Plan travel times carefully when combining distant places, and choose a route that leaves space to enjoy each stop.

Places to explore

Maharashtra highlights

Mumbai

Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta Caves, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus and Juhu Beach.

Ajanta & Ellora

Explore the Ajanta Caves and Ellora Caves, including the remarkable Kailasa Temple.

Mahabaleshwar

Venna Lake, Arthur's Seat, Mapro Garden and viewpoints over the Western Ghats.

Pune

Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace, Sinhagad Fort and the city's museums and gardens.

Matheran

Explore a vehicle-restricted hill station with forest walks and scenic viewpoints.

Lonavala & Khandala

Bhushi Dam, Karla Caves, Lohagad Fort and monsoon-season hill views.

Shirdi

Visit Shri Saibaba Samadhi Mandir and nearby Shani Shingnapur on a pilgrimage trip.

When to go

October to March suits many circuits. The monsoon can bring lush hill scenery; check local weather and site conditions.

Good to know

Distances between major highlights can be significant. Allow time for road or rail transfers when planning a multi-stop tour.
