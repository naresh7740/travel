A closer look

A classic Nilgiri hill station

Ooty, also known as Udhagamandalam, is a hill-station base for gardens, viewpoints and nearby tea-country scenery. Spread out visits to avoid rushing between attractions.

Road and rail services can be busy during holidays. Confirm train schedules, entry hours and local transport for your dates.

Places to explore in Ooty

Government Botanical Garden

Walk landscaped gardens and see plant collections; check current opening information.

Ooty Lake

Enjoy the lakeside and boating options where available and operating.

Doddabetta Peak

Visit the high viewpoint when roads and weather allow; visibility varies with conditions.

Nilgiri Mountain Railway

Check official route schedules and seat availability for this heritage train journey.

Hill-station weather

Carry a light warm layer and rain protection; conditions can shift during the day.
