Rajasthan, place by place

From Jaipur's grand forts to Jaisalmer's desert dunes, Rajasthan is full of cities, historic sites and nature escapes. Here are popular places to help you start planning.

You can focus on one area or combine nearby stops. Distances are long, so allow enough time for travel between cities.

Cities, forts & experiences

Places to visit in Rajasthan

Jaipur

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Jaigarh Fort, Nahargarh Fort, Jal Mahal and Albert Hall Museum.

Jaisalmer

Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake, Bada Bagh, Kuldhara village and the Sam Sand Dunes desert experience.

Jodhpur

Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada, Umaid Bhawan Palace, Mandore Gardens, Toorji Ka Jhalra and the Blue City lanes.

Udaipur

City Palace, Lake Pichola, Jag Mandir, Fateh Sagar Lake, Saheliyon Ki Bari and Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace).

Bikaner

Junagarh Fort, Rampuria Havelis, Lalgarh Palace and the Karni Mata Temple at Deshnok.

Mount Abu

Dilwara Temples, Nakki Lake, Guru Shikhar, Achalgarh Fort and Sunset Point.

Ajmer & Pushkar

Ajmer Sharif Dargah, Ana Sagar Lake, Pushkar Lake, Brahma Temple and Savitri Temple.

Chittorgarh

Chittorgarh Fort, Vijay Stambh, Kirti Stambh, Rana Kumbha Palace and Padmini Palace.

Ranthambore

Ranthambore National Park and its historic fort. Safari access is controlled and should be booked in advance.

Bundi

Taragarh Fort, Bundi Palace, Chitrashala and the city's historic stepwells.

Kumbhalgarh & Ranakpur

Kumbhalgarh Fort and the celebrated Jain temple complex at Ranakpur make a rewarding heritage trip.

Barmer

Barmer Fort, Kiradu Temples, local handicrafts and the nearby desert landscape.

When to go

October to March is generally comfortable for many Rajasthan journeys. Summer can be very hot; check local weather before planning.

Plan your route

Ranthambore safaris, fort visits and seasonal events may need advance planning. Check current opening times, access and booking requirements.
