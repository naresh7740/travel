Salt desert, heritage and craft

The Kutch region combines vast open landscapes with towns, historic sites and distinctive craft communities. Distances between stops can be substantial, so plan a practical route.

White Rann access and seasonal events may require permits and can change by date. Verify current visitor rules, event schedules and local conditions before booking.

Places to explore in Kutch

Great Rann of Kutch

Visit the salt desert area only when access is open and with any required visitor permission.

Bhuj

Use the city as a base for regional museums, palaces, markets and nearby craft villages.

Dholavira

Explore the archaeological site and museum area; check current opening information and road access.

Mandvi

Discover the coast, historic shipbuilding area and Vijay Vilas Palace surroundings.

Seasonal access

Check current permit rules and event timings for the White Rann; do not rely on previous years' schedules.

Long distances

Arrange transport and overnight stops in advance, and carry water and sun protection for open desert areas.
